**Figure X. Architecture of AFBMS-ResUNet.** (a) The model retains the residual encoder–decoder backbone and adds three complementary components. A fixed 3D box filter produces a high-frequency image residual, and the LHFC branch supplies four scale-matched feature maps to the decoder concatenations. AR-MSC V2 operates after the original bottleneck residual stage. The segmentation and auxiliary boundary heads share the final decoder representation. (b) LHFC comprises Conv3D–GroupNorm–ReLU units and average-pooling transitions, producing 24, 24, 48, and 96 channels at full, half, quarter, and eighth resolutions, respectively. Decoder fusion concatenates the upsampled tensor, encoder skip tensor, and corresponding high-frequency tensor before a residual block. (c) AR-MSC V2 uses four parallel branches at the same bottleneck resolution: three dilated 3×3×3 convolutions with dilation rates 1, 2, and 3, and one 1×1×1 convolution. Branch outputs are concatenated, projected using a 1×1×1 convolution and group normalization, and added to the input through a learnable scalar initialized to zero. (d) ABS learns annotation-derived 3D inner boundaries through an auxiliary head and binary cross-entropy with logits. The total objective combines the baseline segmentation objective with the auxiliary boundary objective using a fixed weight of 0.1. Native MRI and GT illustrations demonstrate the operators; loss targets during training are generated after dataset preprocessing. Feature/output tensors are schematic. RB, residual block; C, channel concatenation; GN, group normalization; BCE, binary cross-entropy. K = 1 for UCSF and K = 3 for BraTS WT/TC/ET.
